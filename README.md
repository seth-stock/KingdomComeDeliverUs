# Kingdom Come: Deliver Us

A free, unofficial co-op mod for **Kingdom Come: Deliverance** (the first game): see your friends in the world, talk to them, share the
world's time, and, when the host's story puts them "on rails", **choose whether to join or to stay in the open world**.

> **This is a modified version, not the official "Kingdom Come: Together".** It is a port of that project to the first game.
> The official repository of the project it derives from: https://github.com/DeepFriedDepp/KingdomCome-Together
> Not affiliated with or endorsed by Warhorse Studios, Deep Silver or PLAION. See `NOTICE` and `AUTHORS`.

Status: **work in progress, never played by two people.** Read `docs/KNOWN-LIMITS.md` before you promise anything to a friend.
